Food Service Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. When the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and log every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Iowa City Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Iowa City Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes.
